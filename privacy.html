Privacy note

How your information is used.

Plain and short. This assessment exists to help the team plan your project before a call or visit.

What we collect

  • Your contact details (name, and an email and/or phone number).
  • Your answers about the project — situation, property, scope, timing, and who's involved.
  • Any guided photos you choose to add.

How we use it

  • To understand your project and prepare for a call or in-home consultation.
  • To contact you about next steps.

Your information is used only for planning your project. We don't sell it or share it for advertising.

Photos

Photos help the team plan the work. Please add only photos of the spaces involved — don't upload documents, ID, or anything you wouldn't show the team in person.

Where it's stored

Submissions and photos are stored securely with our hosting provider and are visible only to the team, behind a passcode. They are not posted publicly.

Access & deletion

Want a copy of your information, or want it deleted? Contact the team using the email or phone number they gave you, and they'll take care of it.
