Readings

An hour of looking, with a frame.

A reading is a scheduled conversation. You bring a question, a pattern, or a stretch of life that will not sit still. We look at it together, then we stop.

What it is not

This is not psychotherapy, medical advice, legal counsel, or a fortune. We will not diagnose you. If the conversation belongs in a clinic, we will say so and stop.
